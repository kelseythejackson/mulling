import sizes from '../../../data/sizes.json';

```vue
<script setup>
import { Mulling } from 'mulling/vue';
defineProps(['status']);
</script>

<template>
  <Mulling :state="status" />
</template>
```

- **Props:** the `mount()` options, kebab-case in templates (`reduced-motion`, `max-fps`). See [Options](../../reference/options/).
- **Attributes:** `class`, `style` and `data-*` go on the wrapper element; `aria-*` attributes go on the loader's labelled element.
- **Template ref:** gives you `update()`, `refresh()` and `element`.
- **Server rendering:** renders a correctly sized placeholder on the server.

For the smallest bundle, use `mulling/vue/core` and register loaders with `use()`: {sizes.vueCore} plus your loaders, not counting Vue.
